Snack stand, dungeon level 3 east

Goblin Union Break

You run the dungeon's only snack stand. Your goblin crew builds the orders, the adventurers are starving, and the union wants its breaks. Keep the line moving and the crew happy.

Best shift: 0

How to play

  1. Read the order. Each customer wants certain ingredients, and later ones add a special instruction.
  2. Tap ingredients to add them to the plate. Tap again to take one off. Pick the special instruction if the order has one.
  3. Press Serve before the order timer runs out. Ingredients and special must both match.
  4. Press Break to pause the timers for 5 seconds and lift morale. You get 3 breaks per shift.
  • Correct orders earn points, with a time bonus. Streaks of 3 or more add a streak bonus.
  • Wrong or missed orders cost points and morale, and break your streak.
  • Quick serves and breaks raise Union Morale. If it hits zero, the goblins walk out.
  • The shift ends after 10 orders, when the 4 minute shift clock runs out, or on a walkout.

Keyboard: Tab and Shift+Tab move between controls. Enter or Space presses a button. Arrow keys change the special instruction.
